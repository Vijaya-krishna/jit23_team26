Mathematical Modeling & System Formulations

1. Non-Linear Server Power Scaling

Real-world processor CMOS characteristics exhibit dynamic curvature modeled with an exponential factor $\alpha \approx 1.4$:

P(u) = P_idle + (P_max - P_idle) × u^α

Where $u \in [0.0, 1.0]$ represents instantaneous CPU utilization, $P_{\text{idle}} \approx 95\text{--}140\text{ W}$, and $P_{\text{max}} \approx 320\text{--}480\text{ W}$.

2. EADCS Multi-Objective Cost Heuristic

For candidate server $s$ and incoming task $t$, allocation is determined by minimizing:

C(s, t) = α · [ ΔE(s, t) / E_norm ] + β · [ (AFT(s, t) - Arrival(t)) / T_norm ] + Penalty_wake(s)

Where $\Delta E(s, t)$ is marginal integrated energy draw, $\text{AFT}(s, t)$ is Actual Finish Time, and $\text{Penalty}_{\text{wake}}(s)$ penalizes waking cold nodes from sleep state.

3. Sleep State & Break-Even Amortization

To avoid thrashing (waking energy cost $E_{\text{wake}} = 1750\text{ Joules}$), servers only sleep when idle duration exceeds:

T_break_even = E_wake / (P_idle - P_sleep) ≈ 1750 J / (100 W - 10 W) ≈ 19.4 seconds
